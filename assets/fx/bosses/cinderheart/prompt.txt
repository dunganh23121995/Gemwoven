EFFECT DESIGN: a heart-of-the-volcano eruption: a glowing spiral magma core (a yellow-orange swirl like the boss's chest) at the centre ruptures and hurls out heavy chunky black basalt boulders with bright orange lava cracks, sharp obsidian horn shards, and thick globs and splashes of molten yellow-orange lava with dark crust; at the peak a ring of cracked lava boulders and lava splashes around the blazing spiral core; then it breaks into tumbling glowing rock chunks, cooling lava droplets and black cinder bits. Mostly rock and molten lava, very few flames.

PALETTE: charcoal black and dark brown basalt, glowing orange, yellow and white-hot lava cracks and splashes, deep red-orange shading, glossy black obsidian.

TIMING: frame 1 a small bright seed/spark at the exact centre of the cell (about 15% of the cell); frame 2 rapid expansion (about 35% of the cell); frame 3 further expansion (about 50%); frame 4 the PEAK: the brightest, densest and most detailed frame, but only about 10% wider than frame 3 (about 55-60% of the cell), still fully inside the safe box with clear magenta space around it; frame 5 the burst breaks apart into large opaque fragments flying outward; frame 6 fewer, smaller fragments scattered and shrinking, almost gone. The burst stays radially centred on the cell centre in every frame. HARD SIZE LIMIT: no frame, including its flying fragments, is wider or taller than about 320 pixels inside its 512x512 slot, so there are always about 90 pixels of empty magenta between the effect and every slot edge; the peak is only moderately bigger than frame 3. In canvas pixels: top-row frames stay within y=90..420 and bottom-row frames within y=604..934; the three columns stay within x=90..420, x=602..934 and x=1114..1446. Nothing is drawn near the horizontal middle line y=512 or the vertical lines x=512 and x=1024.

STYLE: OPAQUE solid painted shapes with crisp edges and cel-shaded highlights, clean HD painterly anime game VFX for a mobile fantasy RPG, NOT pixel art. No translucent haze, no soft smoke, no mist, no blurry glow halos, no semi-transparent gradients fading into the background; any glow is painted as a solid opaque colour shape. No character, no creature body, no boss, no text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no borders, no grid lines, no ground plate, no shadow.
Everything stays fully inside its own cell with at least 15% magenta padding on all four sides; no fragment, spike or spark crosses a cell edge or enters a neighbouring cell. Background 100% solid flat #FF00FF magenta, no gradient. Never use magenta, pink or hot pink anywhere in the effect, not even in the glow, and no purple or violet.
