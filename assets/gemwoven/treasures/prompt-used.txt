Rows (top to bottom):

3. "Khắc Chế" (counter seal): a round talisman seal with a chain and a crossed-rune emblem.
Colour each item's gem/enamel by its column's quality colour; metal stays gold/silver.

Rules: one object per cell, centered, inside the central 70% of the cell, nothing crossing cell edges, no text, no letters, no numbers. Background: solid flat #FF00FF magenta in every cell, no grid lines, no shadows on the background.
