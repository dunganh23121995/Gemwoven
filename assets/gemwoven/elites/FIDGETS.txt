Foe idle motion recipe (approved by the user on the pilots: forest-rootknight, fire, mire)

Tool: python assets/gemwoven/elites/fidget.py (read its docstring). Art only from Codex (codex_image_gen.py via
the tool); never stretch, warp or squash a master in code: every moving frame is a real Codex drawing.
Model ladder: start gpt-6.1-sol low; escalate (sol medium, sol high, gpt-6-astra medium, gpt-6-astra high) only when
the result fails review. Generate sequentially.

ELITES AND BOSSES: keep their current calm idle; add TWO occasional actions ("fidgets") each, designed for THAT
character from its master image and brief: something it would do while waiting for battle, using what it has
(weapon, shield, staff, wings, tail, claws, a glowing core, a lantern, a book, a hat...). Examples the user approved:
  knight: "brings the shield forward and taps the mace on it twice" / "rests the mace on his shoulder, holds, lowers"
  dragon: "the lava cracks on the chest flare bright then cool" / "spreads its wings wide, holds, folds them"
  mushroom witch: "taps her staff on the ground twice, the spore bulb pulses" / "straightens her mushroom cap"
Write the action as frames 2-7 (frame 1 and 8 = rest), name the parts that move, say everything else (body, legs,
face) stays exactly as in Image 1. Keep motion local; whole-body moves look wrong. Prefer a weapon/limb gesture plus a
glow/element accent, and make the two fidgets different in kind.
  gen   python fidget.py gen <id> <name> sol low "<what it is>" "<action>"
  proc  python fidget.py proc <id> <name>            (add --whole only for big moves where Codex shifts the body or
                                                       head too, e.g. wings opening wide: then frames are used whole)
  check READ .build-cache/fidgets/<id>/<name>/fidget.webp (2x4, 256 cells). Reject and regenerate when:
        a part appears twice (old pose left behind = ghost), the face/head is distorted or redrawn differently,
        the body jumps in size or position, a frame is cut off, the action is not what was asked, colours drift.
        Look at the face at 2x zoom. A ghost head/limb with normal proc means: try --whole, or regenerate.
  accept python fidget.py accept <id> <name> "<Vietnamese label>" [--whole]
  The tool replaces clipped/misplaced frames by held ones; a fidget where more than two frames were replaced is
  rejected (regenerate).

COMMON FOES: one looping 8-frame idle each, lively like Motocombat battle monsters: a squash-and-stretch hop,
bob or sway that suits the creature (slime squishes and bounces, bat flaps and hovers, bird bobs and ruffles,
crab sways side to side with claws up, plant creature sways, ghost floats). Frames are whole Codex drawings.
  gen   python fidget.py gen <id> idle sol low "<what it is>" "<the loop>" --loop
  proc  python fidget.py proc <id> idle --whole
  check same rules (no size jumps beyond the squash, no redesign between frames, smooth loop)
  accept python fidget.py accept <id> idle "Nghỉ"
Master images: assets/gemwoven/items/creature-<id>.png, or monster-<id>.png for slime, acorn, bat.
