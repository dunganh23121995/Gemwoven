Reference image 1 is the approved mockup of the hero screen of a cozy hand-painted chibi fantasy mobile RPG. Look at its six equipment sockets beside the heroine (Mũ, Áo, Vũ khí, Kính, Quần, Giày).

Draw SIX separate equipment socket frames in exactly that painted style, as game UI pieces, arranged in a 3 x 2 grid with wide empty space between them (no piece touching another or the canvas edge).

Every piece has the SAME shape and size: a square socket about 300 x 260 px: a thick aged-brass frame with small rivets at the corners, a deep dark-brown leather hollow inside (soft inner shadow, empty), and directly attached under the socket a small dark wooden footer plaque about 280 x 70 px with a thin brass edge (the slot name will be written on it later). Front view, flat, no perspective.

The six differ ONLY in the coloured inlay running along the inside of the brass frame and two small cut gems on the frame's top and bottom middle:
1 (top-left): neutral: no coloured inlay, plain warm brass, the gems are plain brass studs.
2 (top-middle): GOOD: soft leaf-green enamel inlay, green gems.
3 (top-right): RARE: sky-blue enamel inlay, blue gems.
4 (bottom-left): EPIC: violet enamel inlay, purple gems.
5 (bottom-middle): LEGENDARY: amber-gold enamel inlay with a faint warm glow, amber gems.
6 (bottom-right): DIVINE: coral-red enamel inlay with tiny gold filigree, red gems.

The leather hollow and the footer plaque must stay completely EMPTY: no items, no icons, no text, no letters, no numbers. Matte painted materials, crisp silhouettes, no neon outlines, no glow halo outside the frame.
Canvas 1536 x 1024 on a solid flat pure magenta #FF00FF background; the magenta must not appear inside the pieces.
