**Top two: concrete build proposals**

**1. A short, skippable onboarding path — no additional economy.**

- Target the first **10 minutes**, beginning only on an ordinary Hiro-only save.
- Sequence: **open stage 1 → highlight one legal swap → finish/retry the fight → claim existing Ghé thăm reward → increase Hiro one level → claim existing Tân Thủ day-1 reward → optionally summon once with a ticket → equip an obtained item if available → return to the next stage**.
- Never guarantee a hero or equipment drop. Skip the equipment step when no item exists. Teach skill training contextually at level 10 and forging at stage 20.
- Show one short instruction and one target at a time. Persist completion from successful actions; support skip, reload/resume and replay without repeat rewards.
- Draw a guide plaque, arrow and target surround with ImageGen; reuse the approved Mèo illustration and existing hero art. Keep explanations behind a tap.
- Tests: fresh save remains Hiro-only with zero Đá Ngọc; guided/manual action sequences produce identical rewards and stats; defeat, absent equipment, reload and skipped steps remain usable. Review phone and Studio **1920×911**, both **≥8/10**.
- Payments and online systems retain **Sắp ra mắt**. Measure local tutorial completion and reaching the next stage; do not claim retention improvement without data.

**2. A complete 28-day calendar — zero net reward increase.**

- Put it in **Sự Kiện → Lịch 28 ngày**, using the existing season anchor and **00:00 GMT+7** reset.
- Each day exposes the **existing** visit reward: **500 gold, 10 EXP books, 80 seals, one summon ticket**, subject to existing seal caps.
- Calendar and Ghé thăm use **one `claimDaily('login')` transaction**. Either entry point completes both. Preserve its single weekly-quest increment and single **50 pass-point** award.
- Full attendance remains **14,000 gold, 280 books, 2,240 listed seals and 28 tickets per season**. Add no extra day-7/14/21 currency.
- Preserve the existing **day-28 20-shard chest**, eligibility and permanent-voucher fallback. Do not require 28 consecutive logins. Ordinary missed daily rewards do not gain a new catch-up entitlement.
- Draw four seven-day strips, reward wells and claimed/locked stamps; use HTML dates/counts. Tap for details. Keep the existing seven-day newcomer rewards distinct.
- Tests: duplicate entry-point claims, midnight/season boundaries, late joining, migration, clock rollback, seal caps, unchanged pass/weekly progress, and day-28 voucher behavior. Verify the complete 28-day economy against baseline.
- No paid catch-up, Đá Ngọc or server-time claims. Trusted time and production settlement remain **Sắp ra mắt**.

Both builds should keep settlement in `progression.js`, pass the runtime suite, and preserve approved Hiro combat coefficients.

I would reject choosing signature weapons or sets first: they add another power layer before the first-session guidance gap is closed. I would also reject calling the day-28 chest a complete login calendar, treating paid Diệu Ngọc rewards as its free replacement, presenting existing titles/gem drops/element warnings as new work, or promoting a local event editor into an authenticated live-ops system.
