Creature and boss voices from real recordings (2026-10-24; replaces the earlier
processed boss creature voices). Every common foe speaks with the voice of what
it is, one family per species group (creature-<group>-attack1..3, hurt1..3, cast,
release, death): slime, bat, rodent, songbird, gull, owl, raptor, crow, rabbit,
fox, wolf, bigcat, boar, reptile, frog, serpent, crab, aquatic, beetle, skitter,
moth, plant, rock, imp, ghost, mech. Casting: voice-casting.json ("creatures";
the old creature-<element> banks stay as fallback). Monster bosses use the same
method (boss-<name>-*). Each clip is one excerpt of one recording, never a mix
of animals. Processing: excerpt, speed change of at most -13 % (bosses only, for
size), high-pass 35-200 Hz, silence trim, short fades, loudness normalisation
(rms ~0.15, peaks < 0.95 through a gentle peak limiter). No layering, saturation,
denoise, synthesis, TTS, voice cloning or AI audio.
Dragons use designed dragon-roar sound effects as published by their sound
designers (Dreeme: built from real lion, dog, horse, elephant and flamethrower
recordings; JoelAudio; AMWD/Stijn Loyen; 999999990; Breviceps). Undead bosses
and ghosts use performed CC0 creature voices.
Clips: creatures/creature-<group>-*.wav, bosses/boss-<name>-*.wav. Exact source
windows, speed and checksums: creature-manifest.json, boss-voice-manifest.json.
Build: python scripts/prepare_creature_sfx.py (after prepare_creature_voices.py);
python scripts/prepare_boss_voices.py (after prepare_cast_audio.py, which
rewrites the manifest with the elemental fallback entries only).
Sources: Freesound uploads, each kept in sources/freesound/<id>-<title>/ with
source.json (title, author, URL, licence, SHA-256), downloaded as Freesound public
HQ MP3 previews. All are CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/;
no attribution required, credited here anyway).
